About Me

Hi! I'm Niezl Skye B. De Torres, a 4th year BSIS student and a web developer focused on building clean, responsive, and user-friendly websites.

I specialize in creating modern web solutions that are not only visually appealing but also functional and easy to maintain.

I enjoy turning ideas into real, working systems from simple business websites to more complex platforms with features like inventory systems, POS integration, and online ordering.

I use technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Django (Python) to develop efficient and scalable web applications.

This portfolio also highlights website samples I built for real-world use, including a Filipino catering site, a premium catering concept, resort and travel websites, and a complete coffee shop system.

Each project was designed with a specific goal in mind: make the layout clear, the experience easy to use, and the content aligned with the business behind it.
